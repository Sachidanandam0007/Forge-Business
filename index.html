    (Good questions)

    Before you ask.

    What do I need to send to get started?

    For a portfolio: your resume, a photo, your projects and achievements. For a business site: your services, photos, contact details and any logo you have. For an app or AI agent: a short description of the idea, or the questions your customers ask most. We structure everything else.

    How long will it take?

    It depends on the package. You get a clear delivery date with your quote before you pay anything. Need it sooner? Pick Express in the quote builder.

    What can an AI agent actually do for my business?

    Answer common customer questions instantly, collect leads, book appointments and pass serious enquiries to you on WhatsApp, day and night. The Forge AI assistant on this page is a live example: try it.

    Do you build apps for Android and iPhone?

    Yes. Starter covers one platform (web or Android), Growth adds a second, and Best-in-Market covers web, Android and iOS, including store submission.

    Is ad spend included in the marketing plans?

    No. Ad budgets are paid directly to Google or Meta, so you always control how much you spend. Our fee covers strategy, creatives, setup and management.

    Can I combine services?

    Yes, most clients do. Build a combined package in the quote builder, then send it to us on WhatsApp and we will reply with a fixed price.
